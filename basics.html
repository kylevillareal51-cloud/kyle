Badminton basics

Everything a new player needs to understand a match and play a first game.

The court and scoring

Court size
13.4 m long and 6.1 m wide for doubles; singles uses a narrower 5.18 m width.
Net height
1.55 m at the posts and 1.524 m at the centre.
Scoring
Rally scoring. A game goes to 21 points and you must win by 2, up to a cap of 30.
Serving
Serve diagonally, underhand, with the shuttle struck below the waist.

Six strokes to learn first

Serve

Starts every rally. A low serve is safe for beginners.

Clear

A high, deep shot that sends the opponent to the back of the court.

Drop shot

A soft shot that falls just over the net.

Smash

A powerful downward shot and the main way to finish a rally.

Drive

A fast, flat shot that travels just above the net.

Net shot

A gentle shot played close to the net that tumbles over the tape.
